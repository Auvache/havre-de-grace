Question 1 · how the lyrics are written

Three lyric styles on the album screen

All three run off Ivory’s real timings, line by line: each line writes itself across the time it is sung, holds, then fades. Muted, the film keeps looping and the words follow a stand-in clock; press play and the words lock to the audio. The loop and the words are independent, so the loop can be any length.
