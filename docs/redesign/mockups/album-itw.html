INTO THE WILD

Havre De Grace · 17 July 2026 · ten songs · 36:45
Play the record

Into the Wild represents the true beginning of the Havre De Grace project. The songs were selected, organized, and produced to be listened to as a whole body of work. The album explores what music means to the artist and the listener: adventure, creativity, self-identity, and a medium for working through big emotions.
