# Ivory — five blanks

## What it does

He is in the crowd, she is on stage, and he falls for her in an instant; after
the show he goes to talk to her and misses his chance. The film is a guessing
game played for her name, five letters, six tries, three boards on one sheet.
The guesses are the five-letter words he sings, typed in as he sings them and
turned over to show how close they come: black for the right letter in the
right place, blue for the right letter in the wrong place, pencil for a letter
that is not in her name. Between guesses the row he is on spins through
letters, looking for words for her. The red cursor is the one thing that
travels.

The song plays the game by itself: SHORE gets two letters, ASKED (both times he
asks her name) gets nothing, and AVOID ("to avoid the heartache") comes closest
of any real word. He wins the first two boards only in his head, by calling her
Ivory. On the third he forgets how to breathe, a row goes by with nothing in it,
and he runs out of tries. Only then does the answer come up, and on the last
"Ivory" it leaves five blanks: her real name, which he never learned.

Every row as the film plays it is `blanksGames(score)`.
