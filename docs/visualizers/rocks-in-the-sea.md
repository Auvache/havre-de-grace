# Rocks in the Sea — visualizer

Track 8. **No film yet**: [/visualizers/rocks-in-the-sea](/visualizers/rocks-in-the-sea) shows the proposed stills, and this file holds the words for them. The song's core idea, from the brief: *Being called, and choosing to stay*.

The stills are drawn by `tools/video-styles/visualizers/rocks-in-the-sea.mjs` and published with `SITE=1 node tools/video-styles/visualizers/build.mjs`. Every still is a real moment of the record (song seconds below). A chosen idea becomes a film in `shared/video/films/` in the Into the Wild maze format: see [README.md](README.md).

## Five ideas

### A. The Cairn

> I stranded those rocks by the bay

A beach with the rocks out in the sea and a small cairn on the sand. One red stone sits at the water’s edge. The tide is the pull, and the cairn is the people he stays for.

**Moves.** The tide creeps up the sand through verse 2 and the first chorus and washes over the red stone’s foot. When the rocks "call my name", rings spread across the water from them. In verse 3 a stone is set on the cairn for each line: his sister, the things they make, the rocks by the bay. On "I choose to stay" the red stone is lifted onto the top. Then the tide goes out and the rain stops.

**Red.** One stone: at the water’s edge for most of the song, and at the top of the cairn at the end.

### B. Tide Table

> And the rocks in the water have been calling my name

A printed tide chart for the bay: heights in metres, the hours across the bottom, the high-water mark as a dashed line. The red curve is the pull, drawn as a tide.

**Moves.** The curve writes itself across the chart for the whole song, with the water under it hatched in blue. Each tide is higher than the last, and the highest goes over the high-water line on "the sand by the rocks in the sea". It turns on "I choose to stay" and falls to the lowest water of the day as the song ends. HW and LW are pencilled in at each turn as it passes.

**Red.** The tide line and the pen drawing it.

### C. Windscreen

> I can't seem to shake all this rain

The view through a rainy windscreen down a road between trees. Rain gathers on the glass, the wiper clears it on every line, and the rain comes back. Far down the road is one small red light.

**Moves.** The road narrows and the trees close in as the pull builds ("the road slims down"). Lane dashes stream toward you the whole time. The wiper sweeps on each sung line. After "I choose to stay" no more rain forms, and the red light grows into the lit window of a house at the end of the road.

**Red.** The light of home at the end of the road.

### D. The Road Home

> When the road slims down and the trees hide the sound

A map in Andalusia’s manner: a hometown, a road out through the woods, the coast, and the rocks offshore. The road is a double line, then a single line, then a dashed track, so it slims down on the paper as it gets to the sea.

**Moves.** A red route drives out of town and into the trees through the verses, and reaches the coast in the first chorus. It waits there through the break. On "I choose to stay" it turns, and it runs the whole road back home alongside its own track, arriving as the song ends.

**Red.** The route: out to the coast, and the hairpin back.

### E. Undertow

> I'll leave all my things and just drift away

A cyanotype current chart: the bay in solid blue, the current as running white lines, the rocks in black at the right and the sand at the left. One red float is carried on the water.

**Moves.** The current lines run, and they strengthen and bend toward the rocks as the pull builds. The float drifts out with them and its dotted track trails behind it. On "I choose to stay" the whole field turns and runs toward the shore, and the float comes back and rests against the sand.

**Red.** The float, and the dotted line of where it has drifted.
