# Ghost — visualizer

Track 10. **No film yet**: [/visualizers/ghost](/visualizers/ghost) shows the proposed stills, and this file holds the words for them. The song's core idea, from the brief: *Helplessness*.

The stills are drawn by `tools/video-styles/visualizers/ghost.mjs` and published with `SITE=1 node tools/video-styles/visualizers/build.mjs`. Every still is a real moment of the record (song seconds below). A chosen idea becomes a film in `shared/video/films/` in the Into the Wild maze format: see [README.md](README.md).

## Five ideas

### A. The City Through Glass

> The city burns and I’m a ghost

A window, and through it a skyline catching fire one roof at a time. The whole film is on the other side of the glass.

**Moves.** Each “burns” and “fire” in the lyric lights another roof, and flames jump on the words. Smoke hatches the sky more thickly each block. On “I’d hold you close” a grey handprint appears on the pane and stays there.

**Red.** The fires. Nothing else in the room is red.

### B. Amplifier Meter

> Amplifiers just make a noise

The VU meter of an amplifier, filling the frame. The needle is the voice. It throws itself at the end of the scale and the scale doesn’t care.

**Moves.** The needle kicks on every sung word with real meter ballistics and falls back. On “fire” it pins hard in the hatched zone. Ghosts of where it just was trail behind it in grey.

**Red.** The needle.

### C. The Dance Card

> If I had legs, we could learn to dance

A printed dance-step diagram. Inked footprints for the lead go round the floor one step per word, and every partner step is a faint grey outline that never fills in.

**Moves.** A step lands on every sung word, counted one-two-three, round a long oval for the whole song. The oldest steps fade behind, so the dance never ends, and nobody ever dances the other half.

**Red.** The step being taken now: a dashed arrow from the last print to the new one.

### D. The Burning Manuscript

> When I write songs, it’s not by choice

A page of staff paper. The song writes itself onto it note by note from the top while the page burns up from the bottom.

**Moves.** Every sung word puts a note on the staff. From the first “burns” the edge climbs: slowly in the first block, past the middle by “I’d play for you if I had hands”, and onto the last notes by the end.

**Red.** The burning edge, with embers lifting off it.

### E. One Match

> But you’re on fire and I’m a ghost

One match, struck at the first word, burning down for the whole song. Nothing can be done about it.

**Moves.** The flame breathes with the voice and creeps down the stick at a steady rate. The burnt wood blackens and curls behind it, engraved rings of light go down with it, and it goes out after the last line, leaving smoke.

**Red.** The flame and the glowing quick of the wood.
