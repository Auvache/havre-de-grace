`dance-card/story.mjs` (who steps where, and when) and `dance-card/engine.mjs`

## What it does

A printed dance-step diagram, seen from above: one floor, and the footprints
on it. Nobody is drawn; everybody is their feet.

- **The father is the ghost.** A man's shoe in outline, in ghost grey, never
  filled in.
- **The mother**: a woman's shoe (a pointed sole and a round heel, the way a
  dance diagram tells the lady's steps from the gentleman's), solid ink.
- **The two children**: bare feet, a sole and five toes, solid ink. The elder's
  are bigger than the younger's.
- **Red is the fire, the upset.** A child's prints go red when the tantrum
  starts and stamp on the spot, on the beat, with the stamp's shock thrown out
  round them: harder and faster the worse it is. A screaming child who has been
  picked up leaves no prints, so the red is thrown out from the mother's hip
  instead.

The story has two halves, and the film goes back and forth between them.

**When they are calm, he is with them.** He dances the box step with a child
standing on his shoes (small bare feet on his outlines, toes to his heels,
the steps counted 1 to 6). They take turns. They play follow the leader,
stepping into his prints behind him. Ring a ring o' roses. Hopscotch on a
court chalked on the floor, his big outlines hopping through after the small
feet. A dance lesson, the younger on his shoes and the elder dancing her own
small box beside them. Tag. By the last block the elder has learned it, and
dances it with him properly, facing him, her steps the mirror of his.

**When they scream, he cannot help.** The stamping starts; he goes to them,
and the nearer he comes the worse it gets and the fainter his own prints
become; he steps back and it eases. Their mother comes in at the door and
across the floor and takes the child up, and walks the floor with short heavy
steps, the red still flaring at her hip, while his outlines follow a step
behind and never touch. On every "I'm a ghost" all of his prints thin.

The last time, both children go off in the middle of all four of them, toe to
toe. He is thrown back. She gathers them up and it does not stop. He walks all
the way round them, a ring of outlines that stay on the floor and never close
in, while the camera pulls back evenly to the whole floor; then he backs away
to the edge of it and stands there, two outlines side by side, facing them.
End card on the last strike of the guitar.

Old prints fade behind everyone (the maze's trail), so the floor never clogs;
only his last ring stays. The hopscotch court, once chalked, stays to the end.

## Choices made without the user (to confirm or change)

- **Who is which mark**: father = outlined shoe in ghost grey; mother = a
  lady's shoe, solid; children = bare feet. Bare feet were chosen because a
  small shoe on a big shoe read as the big shoe filled in.
- **Red is the tantrum**, not the step being taken (as it was in the still).
  "You're on fire and I'm a ghost."
- **Two children**, an elder and a younger; no genders are shown (the notes
  here say "she" for the elder and "he" for the younger only to tell them
  apart).
- **The games**: box step on his shoes, follow the leader, ring a ring o'
  roses, hopscotch, a dance lesson, tag, the elder as his partner.
- **It does not resolve.** The film ends with the fire still going and him at
  the edge of the floor.
- **The mother comes and goes by a door** in the first half and stays on the
  floor, standing by, in the second.
- The count on the dance is 1–6 (the six steps of the box), the way a printed
  diagram numbers them.

## How it is built

- **The record.** 110 BPM on a click, a beat at 0.25 s, in four (measured
  2026-11-10; see the header of `ghostScore.ts`, which also gained `endCardAt`
  191.3, `album` and `artist`). `bt(k)` is beat k. Nearly every step lands on a
  beat, a half or a quarter; the ones that answer a word land on the word
  (`W(line, word)`). The old still counted "one-two-three"; the song is not a
  waltz, so the box is danced slow-quick-quick (beats 1, 3, 4).
- **The story is a table**, laid out once per score (`buildStory`): each person
  is a `Person` with a list of prints `{ t, x, y, th, foot, … }`. Generators in
  `engine.mjs` write them: `walk` (a line, left-right-left, on a beat grid or
  given times; `side` for walking abreast, `face` for walking backwards,
  `dist` for following behind), `boxStep`, `onFeet`, `partner`, `inPrints`,
  `ring`, `sway`, `tantrum` (rate and size from an intensity curve), `place`
  and `liftAll` (set down, picked up).
- **A frame** only asks which prints exist: a print comes down in 90 ms (a
  stamp from larger, and past its size and back), stays whole while the foot is
  on it, drops to half the moment the foot leaves and fades over its life
  (4–6 s walking, half a second stamping, never for `keep`).
- **How thin he is**: every print of his is thinner the closer it lands to a
  stamping or screaming child (`thinNearFire`), and all of them thin on each
  sung "ghost" (`ghostAt`). Never under about a third, or he cannot be seen at
  all.
- **The camera** is a list of held views with long eased glides (`VIEWS`), and
  one pull-back at the end that is even in scale. The floor is the plate's own
  size, so the pull-back ends with the whole floor filling the plate.
- **The lyric**: the score's four sections run block to block, so by the
  album's rule a block's last line would sit in the margin through the whole
  break. For the lyric only, the film ends each section 1.1 s after its last
  word.
- Cost: about 0.04 ms a frame in node (0.5 ms at worst), 21 KB of SVG at most. No `lib`.

## Soft spots and open questions

- **Sync and motion have not been judged by anyone**: whether the stamps feel
  on the beat (the grid is good to about 20 ms; the score's word times are
  Whisper's and its header lists the lines least sure), and whether a step
  reads as a foot coming down.
- **Is it too bleak, or not bleak enough?** It ends unresolved. An ending where
  the fire goes out and he is let in was not made.
- **How faint the father gets.** He is held above a third so he can be read on
  a phone; the idea (the nearer, the less of him) wants him fainter.
- **The mother's shoe** (sole and round heel) reads as a mark more than a shoe
  at small sizes. The children's bare feet read at once.
- **Busy moments**: where the children run to the hopscotch court (1:45), and
  the walk across to the mother (2:50), three trails overlap for a second or
  two.
- The mother stands still for the whole of the third block, and the elder
  child for most of the worst night. Deliberate, but they could be given
  something to do.
- `ghostScore.ts` is generated by `tools/captions/album-score.py`; the film's
  fields were added by hand and a regeneration would drop them.
