# Ghost — the dance card

Track 10. [/visualizers/ghost](/visualizers/ghost). Film:
`shared/video/films/dance-card.mjs` (`danceCardFrame`, `DANCE_CARD_BEATS`), with
`dance-card/story.mjs` (who steps where, and when) and `dance-card/engine.mjs`
(how feet are laid down and drawn). Score: `app/config/ghostScore.ts`.
v2, 2026-11-17 (v1, 2026-11-10, is in git at `b709b51`). **Not yet seen by the
user.**

## What it does

A dance-step diagram on bare paper, seen from above. Nobody is drawn; everybody
is their feet. There is no room: no floor edge, no door, nothing printed but
the footprints and the count.

- **The father is the ghost, and the one red thing.** A man's shoe in red
  outline, never filled in, red from the first frame to the last. He thins —
  his line gets finer, it does not change colour — on every sung "ghost", and
  the nearer he comes to the child when she is sad.
- **The mother**: a woman's shoe (a pointed sole and a round heel, the way a
  dance diagram tells the lady's steps from the gentleman's), solid ink.
- **The child**: one child, bare feet, solid ink.
- **Sad feet** are the upset (there are no tantrums, stamps or red marks on
  anyone but him): she stops, her feet come together with the toes turned in,
  and she drags herself away a small step at a time, a foot every two beats,
  each step a grey smear along the paper from where the foot was, turning her
  back on him. The prints fade fast; the drag lingers. Then she **runs** —
  quick long strides, a step every half beat — to her mother, and stands
  against her toes, or is picked up (her feet leave the paper and her
  mother's steps rock).

The story: he is alone; she comes running to him; they play (on his shoes,
follow the leader, a ring, tag, the dance); and every time she is sad he comes
to her and it does not help, and she goes to her mother.

1. **Him alone.** The black lifts off two red outlines standing in the middle
   of bare paper.
2. **Little feet** come running in from the left edge (12 s), stop at his toes,
   bounce twice, and climb onto his shoes: the box step, counted 1–6, through
   "Amplifiers just make a noise". She hops down.
3. **Follow the leader** ("When I write songs"): he sets off round the paper
   and she steps into his prints three beats behind.
4. **Sad feet** ("The city burns", 39.0): she stops; toes in; dragged steps
   away. He turns back and comes to her, three slow steps, thinner each one.
   Her mother walks in from the top right. On "ghost" she runs to her.
   He steps back.
5. **He tries again** (the second "burns"): two steps toward them; she drags
   herself round behind her mother, and is picked up.
6. **Back to him.** Set down, she walks to him slowly, then runs. **Ring a
   ring o' roses**: all three of them, hand in hand, round once and a bit.
   Then her mother walks out at the right.
7. **The worst night** ("Give me shelter"): left with him, she drags a few
   steps after her mother. He comes slowly round in front of her, toe to toe
   on "help"; she turns her back on him, and on "breathe" runs the width of
   the paper to her mother, who has come back in at the bottom left. He
   follows and stands off.
8. **Can't pick you up**: her mother sets her down; he steps in on "pick" and
   "up"; on "can't" and "down" she drags herself back to her mother's side.
   **Not allowed**: picked up on "hold"; his outlines go round them at a
   distance, a ring that never closes in.
9. **Tag** ("I'd play for you"): she runs, he chases in big slow steps; her
   mother stands back. **On his shoes again** ("If I had legs"). **The most**:
   she runs rings round him and comes in to his toes on "most".
10. **On fire**: sad feet at his toes; she turns from him, drags away, runs to
    her mother. He follows two steps on "You're on fire" and stops; on
    "ghost" his line goes hairline.
11. **Walking the floor** (the break): her mother carries her round the
    paper; he walks her line four steps behind.
12. **Tag** again, round and round; **the dance**: she has learned it, facing
    him, her steps the mirror of his; **all three**: the two of them walk
    across to her mother side by side and stand toe to toe on "most".
13. **The last time** (177.8): sad feet in the middle of the three; she runs
    round to her mother's far side and is picked up. He is thrown back three
    steps, **walks all the way round them** (outlines that stay), backs to the
    bottom edge and stands, while the camera pulls back evenly to the whole
    paper. End card on the last strike, 3:11.3.

## Choices made without the user (to confirm or change)

- **One child**, not two (v1 had two). One child makes it a story you can
  follow — her and him, and her mother — and "the kid who then runs to mom"
  is one kid.
- **He stays an outline**, red. Filled red read as a different, stronger
  person; the outline keeps him the ghost.
- **He thins by line weight, not by fading**, so he is red the whole time:
  down to about a third of his line on each "ghost" and next to her sadness,
  with a little opacity. An old print of his still fades as it ages, like
  everyone's.
- **What sad feet are**: toes in, feet close, slow dragged steps with a smear,
  turning away from him; a sad print fades in 1.4 s so the pair never piles
  up into a blot, and the smear stays five seconds.
- **The mother comes and goes by the edge of the paper**: in at the top right
  for the first sadness, out at the right after the ring, back in at the
  bottom left for the worst night; on the paper from then on.
- **Ring a ring o' roses has the mother in it** (v1: him and the two children,
  without her) — the one time all three hold hands before the end.
- **Tag replaces the hopscotch** in block 3.
- **It still does not resolve.** It ends with her in her mother's arms and him
  at the edge of the paper, his ring round them.

## How it is built

- **The record.** 110 BPM on a click, a beat at 0.25 s, in four (measured
  2026-11-10; see the header of `ghostScore.ts`, which also gained `endCardAt`
  191.3, `album` and `artist`). `bt(k)` is beat k. Nearly every step lands on a
  beat or a half; the ones that answer a word land on the word (`W(line, word)`).
  The box is danced slow-quick-quick (beats 1, 3, 4).
- **The story is a table**, laid out once per score (`buildStory`): each person
  is a `Person` with a list of prints `{ t, x, y, th, foot, … }`. Generators in
  `engine.mjs` write them: `walk` (a line, left-right-left, on a beat grid or
  given times; `side`, `face`, `dist`), `boxStep`, `onFeet`, `partner`,
  `inPrints`, `ring`, `sway`, **`sad`** (feet together toes in, then dragged
  shuffles, `toe` < 0 and `wide` < 1 on the step), `faceTo`, `place` and
  `liftAll` (set down, picked up). The story's `run` is a `walk` at half beats
  with 1.45 × the stride.
- **A frame** only asks which prints exist: a print comes down in 90 ms, stays
  whole while the foot is on it, drops to half the moment the foot leaves and
  fades over its life. A `scuff` print draws its smear from where that foot was
  as it slides in, and the smear outlives it.
- **How thin he is**: `thinNearFire` (the name is v1's) with the child's sad
  prints as sources, within 2.2 s; and `ghostAt` on every sung "ghost". The
  line never goes under about 28% of its 4.2-unit weight.
- **The camera** is a list of held views with long eased glides (`VIEWS`), and
  one pull-back at the end that is even in scale. Nothing of the floor is
  drawn, but it still bounds the camera, and is the plate's own size, so the
  pull-back ends with the whole paper filling the plate. Anything placed past
  ±766 × ±388 is off the paper: that is where she runs in from and her mother
  comes and goes.
- **The lyric**: the score's four sections run block to block, so by the
  album's rule a block's last line would sit in the margin through the whole
  break. For the lyric only, the film ends each section 1.1 s after its last
  word.
- Cost: about 0.03 ms a frame in node, 15 KB of SVG at most. No `lib`.

## Soft spots and open questions

- **Sync and motion have not been judged by anyone**: whether a dragged step
  reads as dragging in motion (it was judged from stills), and whether the runs
  feel like a child running.
- **Small on a phone.** Her bare foot is 46 units long; the camera comes in to
  2× for the first sadness, but in the wide shots (1.2–1.5×) she is a small
  mark. Sad feet are the thing to check on a phone first.
- **The worst-night run** crosses most of the paper (about 1.5 s of running):
  deliberate (she runs past where he can reach), but long.
- **Busy moments**: the ring (three people on one small circle) and the circle
  round her mother on the second "burns" are dense for a second or two; so is
  the partner dance, where her feet land near his old forward prints.
- **How faint he gets.** He is held to a hairline, never gone; the idea (the
  nearer, the less of him) could take him further.
- `ghostScore.ts` is generated by `tools/captions/album-score.py`; the film's
  fields were added by hand and a regeneration would drop them.
