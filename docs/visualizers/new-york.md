# New York — visualizer

Track 6. **No film yet**: [/visualizers/new-york](/visualizers/new-york) shows the proposed stills, and this file holds the words for them. The song's core idea, from the brief: *Rebellion*.

The stills are drawn by `tools/video-styles/visualizers/new-york.mjs` and published with `SITE=1 node tools/video-styles/visualizers/build.mjs`. Every still is a real moment of the record (song seconds below). A chosen idea becomes a film in `shared/video/films/` in the Into the Wild maze format: see [README.md](README.md).

## Five ideas

### A. The Lights on the Shore

> I don't wanna follow those lights I see out on the shore

A night sea engraved in black lines, with the shore along the horizon and its lights coming on one by one in poster yellow. Under us a boat is heading out to sea, and its wake runs back to the shore in red.

**Moves.** We pull away from the land for the whole song, so the shore and its lights shrink towards the horizon. Every line lights more of them, and they flare on every "lights". Every refusal throws the boat into a hard turn that stays in the wake. By the held "York" the shore is a thin yellow thread.

**Red.** The wake: every turn he made away from the shore.

### B. Against the Grain

> I don't wanna listen to anybody ever anymore

A field of hundreds of small ink arrows, all pointing towards a yellow light on the right edge of the frame. One red arrow points the other way and pushes slowly through them.

**Moves.** The field sways on the beat. Every measured "No", "don't", "won't" and "not" sends a ring out from the red arrow, and the arrows it reaches turn round and go dark with him. Across the song the turned ground spreads behind him and the light fades.

**Red.** The one arrow pointing the other way, and the ring of each refusal.

### C. The Signpost

> They said move to Memphis, Nashville, Austin, L.A., or Detroit

A fingerpost at a crossroads. New York is the top board, in poster yellow, and every city they told him to move to is nailed up under it as it is sung. His own board is on the other side of the post: red, blank, pointing the other way.

**Moves.** Boards go up on their words in verse 2. Every refusal knocks one loose to swing and hang off its nail, and New York shudders on each. His board is painted a stroke per refusal. On the first "York" New York swings loose, and as the voice lets go of the held "York" it drops into the grass.

**Red.** His board, the only one with no name on it.

### D. Out of Time

> I don't wanna listen to anybody ever anymore

Seven engraved metronomes in a row, all ticking at the record's own 98.5. The one in the middle has a red arm.

**Moves.** All seven keep the click together until the first "don't". From there the red one drifts onto its own slower tempo, and its readout drops below 98.5. In the "No, no, no, no" sections it swings hard against the other six. On the held "York" it stops dead and stands upright while the rest tick on.

**Red.** The metronome that will not keep time.

### E. The Chain

> No, I won't go, I'm not going to New York

A chain across the plate, from an iron ring in the wall to a winch marked New York that is winding it in. The link in the middle is red.

**Moves.** The winch takes up slack all song, so the chain rises from a deep sag to a straight bar. Every "No" jerks it and pulls the red link a little further open. On 3:26, as the voice finally lets go of "York", the red link parts: one half drops from the wall, the other is reeled away.

**Red.** The one link that will not hold.
